# No negative Expressions or Poses

Avatars depict real, recognizable children and often end up on a classroom wall, so no Expression or Pose shows a child as sad, angry, in trouble or being told to stop. Teachers will ask for these for behavior charts. We decline, because putting a negative state on a child's own likeness publicly shames them. A chart can still show "needs a reset" through its layout, but never through the Avatar.
