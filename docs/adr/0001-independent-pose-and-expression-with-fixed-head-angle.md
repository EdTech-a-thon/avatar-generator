# Pose and Expression are independent, and every head uses one fixed angle

Any Pose can be combined with any Expression, and every head is drawn at the same fixed angle. We chose this over Bitmoji-style scenes (each one drawn with its own face, body and head angle) because variety comes from multiplying Poses by Expressions instead of paying for every combination in art. A single head angle also means hair, head coverings and glasses only have to be drawn once instead of once per angle. Open Peeps already works this way.

## Consequences

Poses can't turn the head: no profile views, looking over a shoulder or looking up. A Pose that needs a turned head has to be declined or redesigned.
