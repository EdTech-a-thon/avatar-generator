# v1 Cutouts are head-only or bust, never full body

Open Peeps has standing and sitting full-body Poses, but v1 doesn't offer them. They're all drawn with adult proportions, so K–5 Students would look like grown-ups. Only one uses a wheelchair and only four have a prosthetic leg, so some Students couldn't be shown in most full-body Poses. Head-only and bust Cutouts avoid both problems: every bust Pose works for every Student, and chart pieces are small anyway.
