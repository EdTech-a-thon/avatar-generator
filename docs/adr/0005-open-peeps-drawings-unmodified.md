# Use Open Peeps drawings unmodified

Nobody on the team can make new illustrations, and no free library met ADR-0003. So every drawn part comes from Open Peeps (CC0) and is used exactly as drawn: we don't add or change any shapes. This supersedes ADR-0003. Representation is limited to what Open Peeps already contains.

## Consequences

- **Included:** hijab, turban, afro, cornrows, bantu knots, twists, gray hair, glasses, eyepatch, facial hair.
- **Missing, and can't be added:** hearing aids, cochlear implants.
- **Wheelchairs:** exactly one full-body Pose uses one.
- **Prosthetic legs:** only four standing Poses have one.
- **Body proportions:** every full-body Pose is drawn as an adult, so Students look like grown-ups from the neck down.
- **Clothing:** every Pose has clothing drawn in, so there's no outfit choice that carries across Poses.
- **Color:** every drawn part has only one line color and one fill color, so the skin and clothing inside a single part can't be colored separately.
- **Poses:** limited to what Open Peeps drew. There's no thumbs up, raised hand or reading, and some Poses (one holds a knife) have to be left out.
