# Full representation ships in v1, even if that means fewer Poses

The first release must include a real range of skin tones, textured hair, head coverings (hijab, patka/turban), glasses, hearing aids and cochlear implants, facial hair and older looks for Teachers, and wheelchair users. Wheelchair users are the costly part: every Pose needs a seated version drawn, roughly doubling the art per Pose. We accepted fewer Poses in exchange. A class chart where one student can't be represented is worse than a chart with less variety.

## Consequences

A new Pose isn't finished until its wheelchair version is drawn. Pose count is limited by art capacity, not ideas.
